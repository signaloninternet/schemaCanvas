# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect contributors to keep discussion direct, respectful, and technically grounded.

## Expected behavior

- engage in good-faith technical discussion
- critique ideas, not people
- keep review feedback specific and actionable
- avoid harassment, discrimination, or intimidation

## Unacceptable behavior

- personal attacks
- abusive language
- deliberate exclusion
- doxxing or private information disclosure

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block participation when behavior damages the project or community.

## Reporting

Use the security/contact channel defined in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for conduct or safety concerns that should not be public.
